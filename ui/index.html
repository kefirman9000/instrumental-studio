All repeated parts Rebuild everything listed below in one go: best versions, drum hits and repeated bars.

Best versions

When an instrument plays the same part more than once, you can use its cleanest take every time, so the part sounds the same throughout. Each one is added to Review for you to listen to first.

Best hits

Drum sounds repeat hundreds of times. Hits the vocals smeared are filled in from the cleanest identical hit.

Repeated bars

Riffs and patterns an instrument plays again and again. Damaged repeats are replaced by the cleanest one.

Repairability

How badly each instrument was damaged in each section, and how well it can be rebuilt from the same instrument elsewhere in the song. Click a cell to review it.

Kept parts

Parts you keep are never repaired or re-levelled. Drag across the Sections bar above the timeline to keep a part.

Original stereo sides

Parts that use the stereo width of the original song (its sides hold the instruments, not the lead vocal). Drag across the Sections bar to add one.

Muted parts

Silence everything, or one instrument, in part of the song. Drag across the Sections bar and choose Mute, or use the M buttons in the table below.

Levels

Loudness of every instrument per section after repairs. Type a boost or cut in dB to change one instrument in one section; it applies to playback and export.

Your own accept and reject decisions are kept when suggestions are re-sorted.

"Use the original song here" fades out this long before any singing starts and stays out this long after it stops, so breaths, pickup notes and reverb tails never sneak into the instrumental.

Troubleshooting and reset

    
Try these in order. None of them touch your songs, projects or exports.

    
Stop what's runningStops splitting, downloads and anything else in the background.

    
Restart the appCloses and reopens the window.

    
Clear downloaded model filesDeletes the separation model files so they download fresh the next time you split. Fixes a download that got cut off.

    
Reinstall the separation modelDownloads and sets up PyTorch and Demucs again (a few GB).

    
Reset app settingsUpdate and display preferences go back to their defaults.

    
Close the open songBack to the start screen. The project stays saved in Songs.

    

    

  





  

    
The app isn't responding

    
Something in the background is taking too long. Restarting usually fixes it; your work is saved.
